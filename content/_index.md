I am a PhD student (2026-2029) working in Probability and Statistics under the supervision of [Randal Douc](https://wiki.randaldouc.xyz/doku.php?id=world:homepage), [François Roueff](https://perso.telecom-paristech.fr/roueff/) and [Alain Durmus](https://alain.perso.math.cnrs.fr/index.html). My research focuses on Markov chains and Markov processes, with a particular interest in the theoretical analysis of Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms.

I am especially interested in convergence and ergodic properties of Markov chains, asymptotic properties of Monte Carlo estimators, and connections between discrete-time algorithms and continuous-time Markov processes.

Before that, I completed the Master 2 [*Mathematics of Randomness*](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/education/masters-degree/mathematics-and-applications/m2-mathematics-randomness) at Université Paris-Saclay and earned an engineering degree from [CentraleSupélec/Université Paris-Saclay](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/).

Feel free to contact me if you would like to discuss research questions or related topics.
